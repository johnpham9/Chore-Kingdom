# Chore Kingdom Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE]

Chore Kingdom ("the app") is published by Luminae Crown Studio LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your family's information.

## The short version

- We do not collect your data. It never reaches us, and we have no servers that store it.
- There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- No third-party code or services run inside the app.
- Your family's information stays on your devices and, if you leave iCloud sync on, in your own private iCloud storage, which we cannot see.

## What the app stores

The app stores the information you enter so it can work:

- Kid profiles: a name or nickname, an avatar and a color
- Chores, rewards and the days and times they apply
- Which chores have been completed, approved or rejected, and when
- Points, redeemed rewards, streaks and weekly summaries
- Your reminder settings and preferences

You choose what to enter. We recommend using a first name or nickname for kids.

This information is stored on the device. It is not sent to us.

## Parent PIN

The parent PIN is stored in the iOS Keychain on the device, set to be readable only while the device is unlocked and never copied to other devices. We cannot see it or recover it.

## iCloud sync (optional)

iCloud sync is on by default and can be turned off in the app under **Settings, then iCloud Sync**.

When it is on, the app keeps your family's information in step across your devices that are signed in to the same Apple Account. It does this by storing a copy in your **private** iCloud database, using Apple's CloudKit. That copy belongs to your Apple Account:

- It is handled by Apple, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- We cannot access, read or retrieve it.
- It is not shared with anyone else, and devices signed in to a different Apple Account do not see it.

To remove it, delete the app's data from iCloud in the iOS Settings app (Apple Account, then iCloud, then Manage Storage, then Chore Kingdom). Deleting the app from a device does not by itself delete what is stored in iCloud.

If you turn sync off, the app keeps working with the data on that device only.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled **on your device** by iOS. The app asks permission the first time you turn a reminder on. No notification content is sent through our servers, because we do not operate any.

## Widgets

The Home Screen widget shows a kid's chores for today. It reads the same data stored on your device and does not send it anywhere.

## Purchase

Chore Kingdom is a paid app. Your payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, and the app has no in-app purchases.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information.
- We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services of our own.
- We do not use third-party software development kits (SDKs) in the app.
- The app makes no network requests of its own. Any iCloud syncing is done by iOS on the app's behalf.

Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated statistics about the app (such as downloads) through App Store Connect. These do not identify you or your children.

## Children

Chore Kingdom is designed to be set up and managed by a parent or guardian for use by their family, and the parent PIN keeps the management features out of a child's reach. The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all. Information about your children stays on your own devices and in your own iCloud, under your control.

## Backups

Your device backups, whether to iCloud or to a computer, may include the app's data, depending on your own device settings. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

## Your choices and control

- **See or change your data:** open the app. Everything stored is visible and editable there.
- **Delete your data:** delete kids, chores and rewards in the app, or delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy, see the iCloud section above.
- **Turn off sync:** Settings, then iCloud Sync.
- **Turn off reminders:** Settings, then Reminders, or the iOS Settings app.

Because we hold none of your information, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date, and describe the change in the app's release notes.
